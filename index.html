Ionic bonds and atoms

Remember! Cations are positive. Just think: Cats have paws, so cations are PAWS-itive!

Everything in the world is made of atoms. Atoms are like tiny building blocks. Some atoms like to give away their parts. These atoms are called metals. We call them givers. Other atoms like to take parts. These atoms are called nonmetals. We call them takers.

When a giver meets a taker, they make a bond. The giver becomes a positive atom. The taker becomes a negative atom. These two atoms have a strong pull on each other. This pull is called an ionic bond. It holds the atoms together tight.

Science word: The "parts" that move are called electrons. You will learn more about them later.

Cations and anions

Remember! Cation: a positive atom. It has a plus sign (+) because cats have paws!
Anion: a negative atom. It has a minus sign (−).

Opposites pull together. A positive cation and a negative anion pull on each other. That is why they stick together to make a bond.

Science word: Atoms with a charge are called ions. A cation is a positive ion. An anion is a negative ion.

Properties of salts

Remember Ionic bonds are very strong! Because they pull so hard, they make special kinds of matter called salts.
CrystalA shiny, repeating pattern, like salt.
BrittleBreaks easily, like a cracker.
High melting pointNeeds lots of heat to melt.

Table salt is a common ionic compound. Its chemical name is NaCl.
